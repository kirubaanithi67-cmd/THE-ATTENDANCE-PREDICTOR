❓ MATHEMATICAL RULES & GUIDE
📅 SEMESTER TIMELINE:

Runs from 29 Aug 2026 to 29 Nov 2026. Saturdays and Sundays are skipped. Specified holidays are excluded from the class count.

📐 FORMULAS (PER SUBJECT):

• $T$ = Classes held so far (from semester start up to today).

• $R$ = Classes remaining in the semester (today onward to 29 Nov).

• $A = (\text{pct}/100) \times T$ (Classes attended so far).

• Must-attend for 75%: $x_{75} = \max(0, \lceil 0.75 \times (T + R) - A \rceil)$.

• Safe skips allowed: $\max(0, R - x_{75})$.

• Must-attend for 90%: $x_{90} = \max(0, \lceil 0.90 \times (T + R) - A \rceil)$. If $x_{90} > R$, maximum achievable % is $(A + R)/(T + R) \times 100\%$.

💀 IRREVERSIBLE DETENTION:

Triggered whenever $x_{75} > R$. This means even if you attend 100% of all remaining classes, your final percentage will still fall below 75%.
